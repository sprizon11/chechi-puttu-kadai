# Privacy Policy — Chechi Puttu Kadai

**Last updated: 8 September 2026**

Chechi Puttu Kadai ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Sai Logabala OPC Pvt. Ltd.
We run the Chechi Puttu Kadai mobile app, which is used to order food for
delivery in and around Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

This policy explains what information the app collects, why we collect it, who
we share it with, and how you can have it removed.

---

## Information we collect

**Account details**
- Your name
- Your mobile number (used to sign in with a one-time password, and to contact
  you about an order)
- Your email address, if you sign in with Google or provide one
- Your date of birth, used to send you a birthday greeting in the app

**Delivery details**
- Delivery addresses you save (home, office or other)
- Your location, only if you grant location permission, to help you set your
  delivery address and to check that it falls inside our delivery area
- Delivery instructions you provide with an order

**Order details**
- Items ordered, quantities and amounts
- The delivery date and meal time you choose
- Payment method (cash on delivery or online), and the payment reference
  returned by our payment provider
- Order status history

**Hospital and corporate plans**
If you enrol for a hospital or corporate meal plan, we also collect the
organisation name, the name and designation of the person who places orders, an
alternate phone number, and the meals, times, days and dish quantities you
request.

**Support messages**
Messages you send us through the in-app support chat, and our replies.

**Notifications**
A device notification token, if you allow push notifications, so we can tell you
about your order.

**Advertising identifiers**
Your device's Google Advertising ID, together with a record of when you install
the app, open it, and place an order (the order value and number of items, not
the dishes themselves). This is used to measure whether our adverts work. You
can reset or delete this identifier at any time in your Android settings — see
"Your choices" below.

We do **not** collect or store your card, UPI or bank details. Online payments
are handled entirely by our payment provider.

---

## How we use your information

We use your information only to:

- create and manage your account
- take, prepare and deliver your orders
- tell you about the status of an order
- answer your questions through support chat
- send you a birthday greeting in the app
- send order updates and, if you agreed at sign-up, offers on WhatsApp
- measure whether our adverts reach the right people and lead to orders
- keep records we are required by law to keep, such as sales records for tax

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

---

## Consent

When you create an account you agree to receive order updates and offers on
WhatsApp. You can stop these at any time by replying to the message asking us to
stop, or by emailing us at the address below. Stopping offers does not stop
essential messages about an order you have placed.

---

## Who we share information with

- **Google Firebase** — sign-in, database, and push notifications. Your account
  and order data is stored here.
- **Razorpay** — online card, UPI and net-banking payments, when you choose to
  pay online. Razorpay handles the payment details directly; we receive only a
  confirmation and a reference.
- **Google Sheets** — order details (name, phone, items, address, delivery time)
  are copied to a private spreadsheet used by our kitchen to prepare and
  dispatch orders.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)** — your mobile number and order details, in order to send
  you the messages described above.
- **Meta (Facebook and Instagram advertising)** — your device's advertising
  identifier, and the fact that you installed the app or placed an order, along
  with the order value. This lets us see which adverts brought people to the app
  and to reach similar people. We do not send Meta your name, phone number,
  email address, delivery address, or what you ordered.
- **Our delivery staff** — your name, phone number and delivery address, so your
  order can reach you.

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law
requires it.

---

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers using Firebase Authentication and
Cloud Firestore. Access is restricted to our staff who need it to run the
service.

---

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is open.
If you ask us to delete your account, we remove it as described below. Records
we are required to keep for tax and accounting purposes may be kept outside the
app for the period the law requires.

---

## Your choices

- You can refuse location permission and type your delivery address instead. The
  app works normally without it.
- You can refuse notification permission. You will still be able to order.
- You can stop offer messages on WhatsApp at any time, as described above.
- You can limit advertising measurement on your phone: **Settings → Privacy →
  Ads**, where Android lets you delete or reset your advertising ID. The app
  works exactly the same either way.
- You can sign out from the app at any time.
- You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask
  us to correct it, by emailing the address below.

---

## Account and data deletion

To delete your account and personal data:

1. Email **chechiputtukadai@gmail.com** from the email address linked to your
   account, or include your registered mobile number in the message.
2. Use the subject line: **Delete my Chechi Puttu account**.
3. We will confirm your identity and delete your account within 7 business days.

**What is deleted**
- Your profile — name, phone number, email, date of birth and saved addresses
- Your order history and any order reviews
- Payment session records held in the app (not the records held by Razorpay)
- Your support chat messages
- Your notification tokens
- Your sign-in account itself
- Your rows in the kitchen spreadsheet described above

**What we may keep**
Sales records that we are legally required to retain for tax and accounting.
Where possible these are kept without your contact details.

**Deleting only some data**
If you want only part of your data removed — for example a saved address or your
chat history — email us at the same address, with the subject **Delete my data**,
saying what you would like removed. We will action it within 7 business days.

---

## Children

This app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information,
contact us and we will remove it.
